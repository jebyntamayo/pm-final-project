# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A former StreamLine subscriber who cancelled because the growing content library no longer translated into a better viewing experience.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Discover quality films and shows through relevant, hand-picked recommendations without having to search endlessly.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** "I cancelled last year. It became a warehouse. Volume went up, quality of my evenings went down. A competitor emails me two hand-picked films a week and I watch both." (UXR-04)
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** A competitor’s curated email service. This is the main tool. A rival sends him two hand-picked films a week, and he watches both. It replaces StreamLine’s browse-and-recommend function entirely.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Cancels StreamLine (Evidenced). He leaves because the library “became a warehouse” and his evenings got worse.
2. Signs up for a competitor’s curated email (Evidenced). He subscribes to a rival that sends him two hand-picked films a week.
3. Reads the weekly email and picks his films (Evidenced). The curation is done for him, and he watches both.
4. Looks up where each film is streaming (Inferred). He likely uses Google or a lookup site, because a recommended film may not be on a service he already has.
5. Plays the film on whichever platform has it (Inferred). The viewing happens somewhere other than StreamLine.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The moment Tom realizes thousands more titles haven’t given him a single film he trusts, while a competitor’s two picks do.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** I cancelled last year. It became a warehouse. Volume went up, quality of my evenings went down. A competitor emails me two hand-picked films a week and I watch both.
