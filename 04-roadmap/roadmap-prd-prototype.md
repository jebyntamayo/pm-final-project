# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** The Wanderer
- **Primary metric:** Month 1 retention of Wanderers exposed to Spotlight.
- **Moment of misery:** I cancelled last year. It became a warehouse. Volume went up, quality of my evenings went down. A competitor emails me two hand-picked films a week and I watch both
- **Guardrail:** Power Users' sessions per week (currently 4.8×).

## Scoring
| Feature | Value | Effort | Quadrant | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Spotlight Curated Rail | 5 | 2 | Quick Win | Now | A human-picked shelf answers Tom's "hand-picked" need directly and targets the Month 1 retention metric. |
| A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label | 2 | 3 | Time Sinker | Cut | It's an AI explanation, not a trusted human pick, and hover doesn't work on mobile. |
| A3 Hidden Gem Badge | 3 | 1 | Fill-In | Later | It's a cheap quality signal against the "warehouse" feel, so ship it as a tag inside A1. |
| A4 Mood-Based Entry Point | 2 | 3 | Time Sinker | Cut | It adds a login step for a persona who wants less effort and risks Power Users' sessions. |
| A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue | 3 | 5 | Time Sinker | Cut | It's another algorithm, and Wanderers give too little data, so revisit it only after A1 proves curation works. |
| A6 Spotlight Digest Email | 5 | 3 | Major Project | Next | It copies the competitor's weekly email, so scope it to three editorial picks sent to everyone, which cuts effort to about 2. |
| A7 Curator Profiles | 3 | 4 | Time Sinker | Cut | It builds trust over time, but a curator byline on A1 captures most of the value for now. |
| A8 Watch Party (Spotlight) | 1 | 5 | Time Sinker | Cut | Social viewing has nothing to do with Tom's friction and moves none of our metrics. |
| A9 Advanced Filter Engine | 2 | 3 | Time Sinker | Cut | Filters add more searching, the opposite of Tom's goal, and mainly serve Power Users. |
| A10 Offline Download (Spotlight) | 1 | 4 | Time Sinker | Cut | Playback logistics aren't the problem and it does nothing for the "nothing worth watching" moment. |

## Roadmap
### NOW, 3-week sprint
- **A1 Spotlight Curated Rail**, A human-picked shelf answers Tom's "hand-picked" need directly and targets the Month 1 retention metric.

### NEXT, following 1-2 sprints
- **A6 Spotlight Digest Email**, It copies the competitor's weekly email, so scope it to three editorial picks sent to everyone, which cuts effort to about 2.

### LATER, backlog
- **A3 Hidden Gem Badge**, It's a cheap quality signal against the "warehouse" feel, so ship it as a tag inside A1.

### ✂ Cut List
- **A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label**, It's an AI explanation, not a trusted human pick, and hover doesn't work on mobile.
- **A4 Mood-Based Entry Point**, It adds a login step for a persona who wants less effort and risks Power Users' sessions.
- **A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue**, It's another algorithm, and Wanderers give too little data, so revisit it only after A1 proves curation works.
- **A7 Curator Profiles**, It builds trust over time, but a curator byline on A1 captures most of the value for now.
- **A8 Watch Party (Spotlight)**, Social viewing has nothing to do with Tom's friction and moves none of our metrics.
- **A9 Advanced Filter Engine**, Filters add more searching, the opposite of Tom's goal, and mainly serve Power Users.
- **A10 Offline Download (Spotlight)**, Playback logistics aren't the problem and it does nothing for the "nothing worth watching" moment.
