# StreamLine Spotlight: Future-State Journey

**Persona:** Tom, former subscriber (UXR-04)
**Strategy:** Restore brand authority through human-led curation, not mass-market scale.
**Value proposition:** For viewers who struggle to find something worth watching, Spotlight offers curated, easy-to-choose recommendations, because we must lift engagement and retention before viewers switch to competitors.

## Timeline

`1 Win-Back Invitation` → `2 Re-Enter Spotlight` → `3 Choose & Watch` → `4 Build the Habit`

## The 4 Stages

1. **Win-Back Invitation**
   - **User Action:** Opens curator email with two picks → sees StreamLine has taste again
   - **Internal State:** Skeptical but curious: "Is this still a warehouse?"
   - **Pain Point Addressed:** Named human curator → restores trust before he recommits

2. **Re-Enter Spotlight**
   - **User Action:** Taps a pick, lands in Spotlight → skips the 15,000-title scroll
   - **Internal State:** Cautiously hopeful; relieved there is nothing to wade through.
   - **Pain Point Addressed:** One short weekly shelf → replaces endless searching

3. **Choose & Watch**
   - **User Action:** Reads curator note, presses play → decides in minutes, not twenty
   - **Internal State:** Confident: "Someone with taste chose this for me."
   - **Pain Point Addressed:** Vetted picks → evenings improve as the library grows

4. **Build the Habit**
   - **User Action:** Rates film, opts into weekly picks → recommendations sharpen, return becomes routine
   - **Internal State:** Loyal; evenings feel worth the subscription again.
   - **Pain Point Addressed:** Weekly ritual moves in-app → competitor's hold on Tom ends

## 3 Competitive Advantages over the Manual Workaround

The workaround is the competitor's email, plus an availability lookup, plus watching elsewhere.

1. **Pick-to-play in one place:** One tap from curated pick to playback → no availability lookups or platform switching
2. **Curation backed by the full library:** Two picks plus related titles → never stuck after the weekly films
3. **Gets smarter as Tom watches:** Ratings and views refine picks → a fixed email list cannot adapt
