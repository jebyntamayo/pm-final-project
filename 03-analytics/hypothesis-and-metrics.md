## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** "I cancelled last year. It became a warehouse. Volume went up, quality of my evenings went down. A competitor emails me two hand-picked films a week and I watch both."
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** Churn improvement for Wanderers when exposed to Spotlight: 22 points lower than non-exposed Wanderers (Snapshot 3).

This confirms Tom's pain because it shows that the segment he fits responds strongly to hand-picked curation, the thing his competitor provides.
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Tom, 52, The Disappointed Subscriber (Wanderers segment)
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Spotlight removes the friction of Wanderers like Tom facing a 15,000-title library with no trusted, human-picked recommendation, so they default to trending content, get little value from their evenings, and cancel.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** Wanderers like Tom stop defaulting to trending titles and start choosing from a short, human-curated shelf. They should shift from about 18% curated viewing toward a higher share, make faster decisions, and come back more than 1.1 times a week.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** Month 1 retention of Wanderers exposed to Spotlight.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Power Users' sessions per week (currently 4.8×).
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** Decision window: 90 days (three monthly cohorts of exposed Wanderers).

Minimum threshold to proceed: Month 1 retention of exposed Wanderers at 74% or higher, which matches the current Spotlight cohorts, while Power Users' sessions stay within 5% of 4.8 per week.

Scale if both hold for two consecutive cohorts.
Pivot if retention is 68-74% or the guardrail slips.
Kill if retention stays at the Full-Library baseline (63-68%) or Power Users drop more than 5%.
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** I believe that giving Tom and the Wanderers he represents a trusted, human-curated pick, in place of an unguided 15,000-title library, will keep them subscribed longer, as measured by a 9-point rise in Month 1 retention of exposed Wanderers (from about 65% to 74% or higher). I will protect Power Users' sessions per week (4.8×, no more than a 5% drop) and will make a go/no-go decision after 90 days.
